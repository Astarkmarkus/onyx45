ONYX45 Internal Project 001 · Sales Operations / Internal Project

Building a structured B2B customer acquisition workflow.

We developed our own CRM workflow to organize prospect research, sales conversations, follow-ups, opportunities and performance tracking.

001

The challenge

As our prospecting developed, we needed a consistent process to manage companies, activities and next steps. Information had to remain accessible and actionable.

Our approach

We connected company research, activity logging and follow-up scheduling with a sales pipeline and daily work planning.

  • Company and prospect records
  • Sales pipeline and contact history
  • Follow-up and meeting scheduling
  • Daily sales planning and activity reporting

What we built

An internal workflow covering company dossiers, call history, follow-up dates, meeting tracking and operational reporting. Screenshots show the actual frontend with business records withheld, rather than invented customer data.

What this demonstrates

How we structure a process and coordinate its stages, from identifying a prospect to recording the next action. This is our own operating infrastructure, not a client outcome.

What this website uses

This release sets no advertising or analytics cookies. It uses browser local storage for your privacy preference (onyx45-privacy-v1) and, when permitted, your language (onyx45-language). These are not advertising identifiers.

Your choice

Use necessary preferences allows the website to remember your selected language. Continue without saving language removes that language preference. Your privacy choice is still stored so that the notice does not repeat on each visit. Both choices allow you to browse and prepare an enquiry.

Duration and removal

Preferences remain in this browser until you change them or clear website data. The Privacy preferences link in the footer reopens the controls. Clearing browser storage removes the saved choice.

Contact forms and external services

Web3Forms processes contact form submissions for delivery and spam prevention. It receives enquiry details only when you submit the form. External services have their own policies. Hosting providers may use necessary security mechanisms outside the control of this static website.

Future changes

Additional advertising or analytics tools are not enabled by these choices. Before adding nonessential tracking, the implementation and this policy must be updated with any required consent controls.

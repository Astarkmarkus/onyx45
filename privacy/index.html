Operator and data controller

ONYX45 is the business name used by the operator identified by organisation number 20010122-9375. This operator is responsible for personal data processed in connection with enquiries and client relationships. For privacy requests, contact Contact@onyx45.com.

Information you provide

The callback form asks for your name, business email, company, telephone number, service of interest and a description of your needs. We use these details to respond to your request. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

Purpose and legal basis

We use enquiry data to respond to the contact you request and discuss our services. For business representatives, this is based on our legitimate interest in answering relevant business enquiries (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), balanced against your rights and reasonable expectations. Where you personally request steps before entering a contract, Article 6(1)(b) may apply. This request does not subscribe you to unrelated marketing.

Form processing

The website sends submissions directly over HTTPS to Web3Forms for processing, storage, spam protection and delivery to the configured business mailbox. Its infrastructure and processing may involve locations outside the EU/EEA. Web3Forms publishes its processor terms and transfer safeguards in its Data Processing Agreement. The website does not automatically create CRM records.

Hosting and service providers

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Hosting and form providers may process technical information to deliver their services, protect against abuse and comply with their obligations. Their processing locations and any international transfers are governed by their applicable terms and safeguards.

Storage and retention

Enquiries and related correspondence are kept only while needed to handle the request, carry out relevant follow-up and, where applicable, manage an engagement. When no longer needed for those purposes, they should be deleted or anonymised. Records required for accounting or legal obligations are kept separately for the applicable legal period. The static site does not store enquiry content in browser storage or its own database; provider-held submissions and mailbox copies also need to be included in deletion requests and retention reviews.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or object where applicable. Contact Contact@onyx45.com; you do not need to send unnecessary identity documents. You may complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY). We assess requests under the GDPR and explain any legal reason to retain a record.

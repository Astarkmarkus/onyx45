Paid Advertising

Advertising built around your business.

From campaign strategy and audience targeting to launch, optimization and reporting, ONYX45 helps businesses plan and manage paid advertising campaigns built around their goals.

The right advertising channels depend on your audience, objectives and market. We help you select and manage the approach that fits your business.

Request a Proposal

What this can include

We develop a campaign approach based on your objectives, positioning, ideal customers, available budget and the actions you want your audience to take.

We identify relevant customer segments, demographics, interests, search intent and geographic markets to guide targeting decisions.

We assess suitable channels against your objectives, audience and resources, and agree the platform mix before setup.

We prepare the campaign structure, platform configuration and agreed assets, check the setup and coordinate launch.

We plan conversion tracking, agree performance indicators and identify measurement requirements and any technical support needed.

We review available performance data and adjust targeting, budgets and campaign settings within the agreed scope.

We report on agreed metrics, explain what the results indicate and recommend practical next steps.

What we work towards

A clear campaign plan, an agreed budget and reporting connected to your objectives.

Example engagementAn advertising pilot with a campaign plan, agreed launch scope, tracking guidance and a performance review.

Advertising budgets are paid separately from management fees. Results depend on factors including your offer, creative, audience and market; specific outcomes are not guaranteed.

Where applicable, ONYX45 may receive referral compensation from advertising platforms, subject to the relevant terms and disclosures. Any applicable arrangement should be discussed before you commit.

02QUALITY OVER QUANTITY

Lead Generation & Appointment Setting

Build a considered route to decision-makers. We focus on relevance, clear qualification and business conversations worth having.

Request a Proposal

What this can include

We agree which companies and decision-makers fit your offer, including useful qualification criteria and exclusions.

We research relevant target accounts and business contacts, organizing the findings for focused outreach.

We plan relevant messaging and conduct agreed outreach, including cold calling where appropriate and lawful.

Where lawful, we plan relevant business emails and follow-up, respecting applicable privacy rules, objections and opt-outs.

We qualify interest against agreed criteria and coordinate sales appointments with relevant prospects. Any meeting commitments are defined in your agreement.

We organize outreach activity, lead status and follow-up responsibilities, with reporting that shows progress and next actions.

What we work towards

An agreed target profile, clear qualification criteria and a structured view of outreach and follow-up.

Example engagementA B2B prospecting project with a target profile, researched accounts, outreach messaging and organized follow-up.

Outreach must follow applicable privacy and marketing laws. Any meeting commitments, qualification rules and commercial terms belong in a specific client agreement; this website does not promise a guaranteed number of meetings.

What counts as a booking?

A booking counts when a viewing appointment has been confirmed with the relevant landlord, agent or property contact for a property that reasonably matches the search criteria agreed with the customer.

  • A property link alone is not a booking.
  • An unanswered message is not a booking.
  • An unconfirmed viewing request is not a booking.
  • A confirmed viewing appointment counts as one booking.

A booking is delivered when the viewing appointment is confirmed. If the property contact cancels, we arrange a replacement booking.

A confirmed viewing does not mean you will receive or successfully rent the property. Packages cover the included number of bookings, not unlimited searching.

What does Onyx Housing do?

We help with property searches, listing outreach and the coordination of suitable viewing opportunities. You assess the property and deal directly with the landlord, agent or platform.

Do you guarantee I will find a home?

No. We provide housing-search assistance and work to arrange the number of confirmed viewing bookings included in your package. The final rental decision is made between you and the relevant landlord, agent or platform.

What counts as a confirmed booking?

A booking counts when a viewing appointment has been confirmed with the relevant landlord, agent or property contact for a property that reasonably matches the search criteria agreed with the customer.

Is sending me a property link considered a booking?

No. A link, unanswered message or unconfirmed viewing request does not count as a confirmed booking.

What types of homes can you help search for?

We can assist with searches for rooms, studios and apartments in selected European cities and agreed surrounding areas. Availability depends on the location, your requirements and your budget.

Do I pay my rent or deposit to Onyx Housing?

No. Property-related payments such as rent and deposits should be handled directly with the relevant landlord, agent or platform. Our fee is only for the Onyx Housing search service.

Are you the landlord or property owner?

No. Onyx Housing is an independent housing-search assistance service. We are not the landlord, property owner, property manager or a property listing platform.

What happens after I choose a package?

What if the property contact cancels a viewing?

We arrange a replacement booking. Your booking is delivered when a viewing appointment is confirmed for a property that reasonably matches your agreed criteria.

What happens after my included bookings are used?

For Pro customers, additional confirmed bookings can be added for €14 each after the 10 included bookings. For Basic and Plus, contact us about upgrading or continuing the search.

Terms of Service

Onyx Housing, a service by Onyx45, provides independent housing-search and administrative assistance. We provide personalised housing-search and viewing-coordination assistance. We do not own or manage the properties, act as the landlord, or guarantee accommodation or rental approval. We do not collect rent or property deposits on behalf of landlords.

Your service period

Every Onyx Housing package has a service period of 30 calendar days. The service period begins once the customer's payment has been successfully completed and Onyx Housing has received the information reasonably necessary to begin the customer's search.

During this period, Onyx Housing works to obtain the confirmed viewing bookings included in the purchased package: Basic (€29) includes 2, Plus (€59) includes 5, and Pro (€99) includes 10. A valid Creator Code verified by Onyx Housing adds 1 confirmed viewing booking at no additional charge: 3 for Basic, 6 for Plus or 11 for Pro. It does not change the 30-day service period.

Service completion

The service is considered fully performed when all confirmed viewing bookings included in the customer's package, including any verified Creator Code bonus, have been delivered. The service may also end in another circumstance expressly defined in these Terms and permitted by applicable law; no additional termination circumstance is specified here.

Reaching day 30 does not automatically mean that an undelivered package has been fully performed. If the contracted service has not been delivered by the end of the agreed service period, nothing in these Terms excludes or limits mandatory consumer remedies.

What counts as delivery

A confirmed viewing booking counts as delivered when a viewing appointment has been confirmed with the relevant landlord, agent or property contact for a property that reasonably matches the search criteria agreed with the customer.

A property link alone, an unanswered message, a request sent to a landlord or agent, or an unconfirmed viewing request does not count. A confirmed viewing counts once the relevant property contact confirms the appointment.

A confirmed viewing does not guarantee accommodation, rental approval, property availability after the viewing, acceptance by the landlord or execution of a rental agreement.

If the property contact cancels a viewing, we arrange a replacement booking.

Your rental decision

Customers are responsible for reviewing the property, verifying the identity and authority of the person offering it, reviewing the rental agreement and deciding whether to proceed. Rental agreements, rent and deposits are handled directly between the customer and the relevant landlord, agent or platform.

Always verify the property and the identity and authority of the person offering it before transferring money.
